01 · The problem

Jet Shaheds broke the interceptor math.

Cheap propeller interceptors like the $2,100 Wild Hornets Sting changed the price of a Shahed kill. Russia answered with jet-powered Geran-4 and Geran-5 drones, which Ukrainian intelligence says were built to counter those interceptors.

Geran-4 / Geran-5 (jet)500–600 km/h
What Kyiv is asking forAffordable, scalable, fast

Speeds as reported (310–373 mph). The required missile performance figures are confidential. The public form asks for range, launch altitude, Mach speed, max G-load, and warhead and fuze type.

“We need technologies that change the rules of the game in our favor.”Defence Minister Evgeniy Khmara, in an address to Brave1 manufacturers

Khmara named high-speed drones, ballistic missiles, cheap cruise missiles and glide bombs as Russia's main threats. The Ministry's call asks for "affordable systems that scale."

One to kill, one to see.

03 · Terms

Money built for small teams.

Non-repayable

You only repay if you breach the rules. No co-financing is required.

Two tranches

The grant is paid in two instalments. The development clock starts at the first payment.

16–18 months

The time a team has to reach the required readiness level (missile: 16, radar: 18).

Deliver to troops

Winners hand finished samples and technical documentation to a military unit the Ministry picks.

04 · Process

From interest to hand-over.

  1. Express interest

    File through Prozorro by Nov 5, 23:59 Kyiv time. Later filings are not considered.

  2. Compliance check

    Brave1 checks eligibility and ownership documents.

  3. Full application

    A 10-working-day window (missile) or 15-working-day window (radar) opens.

  4. Scoring

    An Armed Forces command and a five-member commission, three of them independent experts, score the bids.

  5. Funding

    The Innovation Development Fund board funds up to 4 projects. The development clock starts at the first tranche.

05 · Eligibility

Foreign capital enters through a Ukrainian company.

Only legal entities registered in Ukraine can apply. A Ukrainian LLC with foreign shareholders qualifies if it documents the whole ownership chain.

✓ Can apply

  • ✓Legal entities registered in Ukraine
  • ✓Ukrainian LLCs with foreign shareholders, with registry extracts for every foreign company in the ownership chain
  • ✓Companies with state or municipal ownership under 25%
  • ✓Teams without a finished product. None is required.

✕ Barred

  • ✕Nonprofits
  • ✕Companies with branches or subsidiaries in Russia or Belarus
  • ✕Firms with sanctioned owners
  • ✕Russian or Belarusian beneficial owners who do not legally live in Ukraine

07 · Analysis

The right bet, with one bottleneck.

DroneXL's analysis of what this call means and what to watch next.

Sized for startups

A cap of about $1.64M per missile grant won't fund a legacy prime. It's aimed at small Ukrainian teams that move fast.

The cost curve, round two

The Sting showed that a cheap airframe can change the price of a Shahed kill. Russia answered with jets, and a drone-carried missile is the next move.

Watch the turbojet call

Brave1 says a turbojet competition comes next. The Geran-4 runs on a Chinese hobby jet that sells for up to $25,000, and engine supply may decide how many missiles Ukraine can fire.
